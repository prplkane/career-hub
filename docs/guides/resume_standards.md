# Resume Standards

Last reviewed: 2026-07-19

Scope: U.S. private-sector technical roles, especially early-career and early-to-mid-career software, implementation, data, and automation positions.

These are defaults, not universal laws. Explicit employer instructions always take precedence.

## Hard Rules

- Every claim, technology, title, date, and metric must be traceable to the Career Vault.
- Never infer missing metrics, years of experience, citizenship, clearance, work authorization, or domain expertise.
- Use a single-column, text-based document with selectable text.
- Keep name and contact information in the document body, not a header, footer, image, or text box.
- Do not use tables, multi-column layouts, graphics, photos, icons, charts, WordArt, or decorative skill bars in the ATS version.
- Use clear standard section names such as `Professional Summary`, `Technical Skills`, `Professional Experience`, `Projects`, and `Education`.
- Preserve company, title, dates, and location as distinct readable text.
- Follow the employer's requested file type and naming instructions.

## Content Defaults

- Tailor the resume to the role's highest-priority requirements using only supported evidence.
- Order experience and education in reverse chronological order unless a role-specific reason is documented.
- Start bullets with a precise action verb and describe the object, technical context, and verified outcome when available.
- Use present tense for current work and past tense for completed work.
- Prefer demonstrated impact over responsibilities, but do not force a metric where none is verified.
- Use exact job-description terminology only when it truthfully describes stored experience.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, copied job duties, generic adjectives, personal pronouns, and unsupported seniority.
- Skills shown in the final resume must be present in the Career Vault and relevant to the target role.

## Selection And Depth

- Every Career Vault experience must be selected or explicitly excluded with a reason.
- A `full` experience receives 3-5 source-backed bullets.
- A `compact` experience receives exactly 2 source-backed bullets.
- Do not include a professional experience as a one-line token mention. Exclude it when there is not enough space to represent it meaningfully.
- Prefer relevant professional experience over a weaker project when both communicate substantially the same evidence.
- Keep older or adjacent experience when it adds a distinct required capability, closes a chronology gap, or materially strengthens the role narrative.

## Length And Typography

- Default to one page for recent graduates when relevant evidence fits without crowding.
- Allow two pages when substantial relevant professional experience would otherwise be removed or made unreadable.
- Never exceed two pages for the current target profile unless an employer explicitly requests a longer format.
- Use one common font, 10-12 point body text, and 0.5-1 inch margins.
- Keep spacing, capitalization, dates, punctuation, and bullet style consistent.
- Remove lower-priority content before shrinking below the minimum typography rules.

## Contact And Privacy

- Include name, city/state, phone, professional email, and LinkedIn URL when available.
- Do not include a full street address in the resume unless specifically required.
- Do not include a photo, age, gender, marital status, citizenship, visa details, references, or sensitive identifiers in the resume body unless the application explicitly requires that information elsewhere.

## ATS And Export Checks

- Prefer `.docx` or a text-based `.pdf`; never submit an image-only or scanned resume.
- Verify that text can be selected and copied in the final file.
- Keep the ATS file below 2.5 MB as a conservative compatibility target.
- After upload, review every auto-populated application field when the platform exposes parsed data.
- Maintain an ATS version separately from any design-heavy portfolio version.

## Human Review Gates

- Confirm every bullet and metric against its source evidence.
- Confirm that every selected technology can be discussed in an interview.
- Check spelling, grammar, chronology, duplicate verbs, and tense consistency.
- Check that the strongest role-specific evidence appears in the first half of the document.
- Review the rendered document at normal zoom and verify that no line is clipped, wrapped awkwardly, or isolated on a new page.
- Keep every generated draft unapproved until the user reviews it.

## Evidence Basis

- [Greenhouse: Unsuccessful resume parse](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse), updated 2026-03-02: parsing risks from columns, tables, headers, footers, text boxes, graphics, images, and unclear sections.
- [Greenhouse: Supported candidate upload formats](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360052218132-Supported-formats-for-resumes-cover-letters-and-other-candidate-uploads), updated 2025-10-27: `.doc`, `.docx`, `.pdf`, `.rtf`, and `.txt` support.
- [Lever: Understanding resume parsing](https://help.lever.co/hc/en-us/articles/20087345054749-Understanding-resume-parsing), updated 2025-11-25: text-based Word/PDF parsing and the selectable-text check.
- [Harvard MCS: Create a strong resume](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/): specific, active, fact-based, tailored, reverse-chronological, readable content.
- [UC Berkeley Career Engagement: Resumes](https://www.career.berkeley.edu/prepare-for-success/resumes/): standard ATS formatting, one-page recent-graduate default, 10-11 point type, and 0.5-1 inch margins.

Review these sources and update `last reviewed` and `career_vault/style_rules.yaml` at least every six months, or earlier if major ATS behavior changes.
